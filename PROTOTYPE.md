# Prototype: still time

Slapjam AI #2 (theme: Speed), mobile portrait browser game.

## Question

When time barely moves while you stand still and runs at full speed while you move, does picking *when* and *where* to move through a room of incoming bullets, arrows and knives feel tense and smart? You clear a room by getting close enough to cut every enemy down (one hit kills, either way); a hit restarts the room.

Variants (V or the bottom button): **Glide** (drag to move, lift to freeze), **Flick** (swipe = a fixed dash; time runs only during it), **Realtime** (no slow-motion: the control).
